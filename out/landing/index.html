Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Monthly, 6-Month, and Yearly plans? +

All features are 100% unlocked in every plan! There are no feature limitations. The only difference is the subscription duration and discount. The 6-Month plan saves you 50% ($15/mo), while the Yearly plan offers maximum savings of 70% ($9/mo).

Do I need a WhatsApp Business API account? +

No. WApp Manager Pro works directly via QR code multi-device pairing (the exact same method used by WhatsApp Web). You can use any personal or business WhatsApp account without paying Meta API per-message fees.

How does the Anti-Ban system protect my WhatsApp number? +

WApp Manager Pro incorporates intelligent human-speed randomized delays (e.g. 5 to 15 seconds between messages), message personalization tags ({{name}}), and staggered dispatching. This mimics authentic human behavior to keep your account safe.

How many messages can I send per day? +

There is no artificial cap imposed by the software. You can send thousands of messages. For newly registered WhatsApp numbers, we recommend warming up with 300–800 messages/day before scaling up to 5,000+ daily.

How do I receive my license key after ordering? +

Click any "Get Key" button to open our instant contact modal. You can connect with our support team directly on WhatsApp or Telegram. License activation is delivered within 5–15 minutes.

Which Windows operating systems are supported? +

WApp Manager Pro runs natively on Windows 10 (64-bit) and Windows 11. It installs as a clean self-contained desktop software without requiring complex server configurations or third-party extensions.
